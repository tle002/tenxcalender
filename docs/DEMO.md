# Demo script (about 3 minutes)

**Setup:** run backend and frontend (`USE_MOCK=true` for the demo calendar, or connect Google). Click **Reset demo** first. Open the Briefing tab.

## 1. The pitch (15 s)
"A manager's week fills up with other people's invites. tenxcalender is a calendar you talk to, and it never changes anything until you approve."

## 2. Intent to ghost events (45 s)
- Type: **Find 45 min with Priya and Sam this week, not mornings.**
- Point out: the dashed ghost on the calendar, the reason on the card, the "Other options" slots, and "How I got this".
- Swap an alternative. Untick, then re-tick the change.
- Say: "Nothing is written yet." Press **Apply**. Show the new event.

## 3. Briefing and one-click fixes (60 s)
- Open **Briefing**. Read the summary and point at the heavy Wednesday bar.
- Open the "Partner check-in lands on your focus time" card. Click **Decline "Partner check-in"**. Show the struck-through ghost. Apply.
- Open the double-booking card and click **Shorten**. Show the hatched tail. Apply.
- Say: "Detection is plain code, so it is fast and consistent. The model is only used to understand what I asked for."

## 4. Undo and Rules (30 s)
- Open **History**. Show the entries. Undo the newest one.
- Open **Rules**. Change focus time to 3 hours per day and save. Press **Protect focus time this week** in Briefing to show new blocks.

## 5. Close (15 s)
"Preview then approve, deterministic maths, validated on the server. Next: meeting prep and team scheduling."

## Backup prompts
- "Protect 2 hours of focus time every day this week"
- "Create a 30 minute meeting this Friday afternoon with a Meet link" (Google mode)
- After a plan appears: "make it Thursday"
